# Investigation

## 1. Frame the question

- State one concrete question whose answer would change a decision or resolve the requested uncertainty.
- Describe what evidence would answer it, including evidence that would disconfirm the leading explanation.

## 2. Gather the evidence

- Gather the identified evidence directly or through [investigation dispatch](../adapter.md#canonical-token-map).
- Preserve source locations, commands or observations, and material limitations so another reader can retrace the path.
- Change the evidence plan when the first results expose a better discriminating test; do not change the question silently.

## 3. Build the argument

Classify every material statement as one of:

- **Observed fact:** directly supported by captured evidence.
- **Inference:** a conclusion drawn from named observed facts; show the reasoning.
- **Open uncertainty:** unresolved because specified evidence is absent, conflicting, or inconclusive.

Do not turn an inference into an observed fact or hide uncertainty inside a conclusion. End with the argument, not a diff: answer the question to the strength the evidence permits, show the supporting fact-to-inference chain, and name what remains open.
